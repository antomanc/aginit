Aginit is an orchestrator and bootstrapper that configures and integrates independent open-source tools and skills. Aginit does not vendor, fork, claim ownership of, or relicense upstream software.

Each integrated upstream tool is governed by its respective authors and licenses:

  - Repository: https://github.com/NanoNets/context-graph-engine

  - Project: Decentralized agent skill package installer

  - Website: https://skills.sh
